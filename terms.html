What Jovian Ring is

Jovian Ring is a habit-tracking app. You choose an officer track (Combat, Engineering or Mining), record your missions, and the app scores them — makes, faults, streaks, a career rank, and medals in a Shadow Box. On top of that it offers optional Friends and Global leaderboards, through Apple's Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android.

Jovian Ring is a work of fiction. The rank insignia it shows are public-domain designs. The app is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any armed service or government.
