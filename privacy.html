Privacy Policy for Jovian Ring

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Jovian Ring is a habit-tracking app for iPhone and Android that turns the goals you keep into a career in a hard-science-fiction setting. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to use the leaderboards, and then only your scores go — to Apple or Google, never to us.

The core fact

Jovian Ring stores everything on your device. Your officer track, your settings, and — once you record missions — your missions, your career record and your medals all live in the app's own storage on your phone. There is no server. Jovian Ring does not have a backend, does not ask you to create an account, does not run analytics, does not use a crash reporter, and does not include advertising software of any kind. Nobody but you can see your data, because nobody but your device has it.

Permissions

Jovian Ring asks for no permissions of its own. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, health data or notifications.

On Android, the app's manifest declares no permissions. Google Play's Billing Library, which the app uses for purchases, brings two of its own (com.android.vending.BILLING and INTERNET) so that it can talk to Google Play. Jovian Ring itself makes no network requests.

What Jovian Ring sends, and to whom

Game Center (iPhone) and Google Play Games (Android). The Friends and Global leaderboards are optional and need you to be signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games. When you are, the app sends five figures from your career record — Success Rate, Career Makes, Big Makes, Stockpile and Incidents — to Apple's or Google's leaderboard service so that you can compare them with friends and other players. Your player name and identity on the leaderboards are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not this one; other players see the display name and score your Game Center or Play Games profile shows. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. On Android, the app can show your Play Games friends only after you tap "Share Friends List" and agree on Google's own screen; until then it does not have that list. Jovian Ring never sees your Apple ID or Google account password: sign-in happens on Apple's or Google's screens.

Purchases. If you buy the full-app unlock or subscribe to Premium, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit) or by Google through Google Play (the Play Billing Library). Jovian Ring never sees or stores your payment information. On Android, the app keeps no record of its own of what you have bought: it asks Google Play each time it starts. Google Play's Billing Library may report its own diagnostics to Google, under Google's privacy policy.

What we collect and store ourselves

Nothing. Jovian Ring has no server to send data to, so there is nothing to collect on our end. Everything described above is processed on your device, by your device, for your own use of the app — except the leaderboard scores, which go to Apple or Google and nowhere else.

Third-party sharing

Jovian Ring does not share your data with any third party, because Jovian Ring does not have your data to share — it never leaves your device in the first place. The exceptions are the ones described above: Game Center and Google Play Games when you choose to use the leaderboards, and the App Store and Google Play when you make a purchase. Those are Apple's and Google's own services and are governed by their privacy policies.

Tracking

Jovian Ring does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use your information for advertising. There is no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK in the app.

How to delete your data

Because everything is stored on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. There is no account to close and no server-side copy to request removal of, because none exists.

Scores you have already submitted to Game Center or Google Play Games are held by Apple or Google under their policies. You can manage or remove them through your Game Center settings on iPhone or your Google Play Games profile settings on Android, not through Jovian Ring.

If you back up your phone — iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone, Android's own backup on Android — the backup may include Jovian Ring's local data, the same way it includes any other app's local storage. That backup is controlled by you through Apple's or Google's backup settings, not by Jovian Ring.

Children's privacy

Jovian Ring is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — because, again, it does not send information anywhere to be collected. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, you can remove the app and its local data at any time by deleting it from the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
